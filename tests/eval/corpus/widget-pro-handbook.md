# Widget Pro — Owner's Handbook

## Overview

The Widget Pro is a portable environmental sensor intended for field use. It
records temperature, relative humidity, and barometric pressure, and stores
readings internally until they are synchronised to a host device.

The unit weighs 340 grams and measures 118 by 72 by 29 millimetres. The housing
is machined aluminium with a moulded rubber bumper, and the display is a
monochrome transflective panel that remains readable in direct sunlight.

## Power

The Widget Pro provides a battery life of 18 hours of continuous logging on a
full charge. Standby time with logging disabled is approximately six weeks. The
battery is a sealed lithium-polymer cell and is not user-replaceable; units
requiring a replacement cell must be returned to an authorised service centre.

Charging is over USB-C at up to 15 watts. A full charge from empty takes about
90 minutes. The unit may be operated while charging, although logging accuracy is
not guaranteed during the first ten minutes of a charge cycle because of the
thermal rise in the housing.

The low-battery warning appears at 15 percent remaining. Below 5 percent the unit
stops logging and enters a protective sleep state, retaining stored readings.

## Environmental limits

The operating temperature range is minus 20 to 60 degrees Celsius. Storage
outside this range will not damage the unit, but readings taken outside it are
not covered by the accuracy specification.

The unit carries an IP67 rating, meaning it is dust-tight and withstands
immersion in one metre of water for thirty minutes. It is not rated for
continuous submersion, and the USB-C port cover must be closed for the rating to
apply.

Humidity readings are accurate to plus or minus 2 percent between 10 and 90
percent relative humidity. Outside that band the sensor remains functional but
the accuracy specification does not apply.

## Storage and synchronisation

Internal storage holds 500,000 readings. At the default logging interval of one
reading per minute, that is approximately 347 days of continuous data. When
storage is full the unit overwrites the oldest readings first, unless
overwrite protection has been enabled in the settings menu.

Synchronisation is over Bluetooth Low Energy or the USB-C connection. A full
transfer of a complete dataset takes around four minutes over USB and closer to
forty minutes over Bluetooth.

## Warranty and service

The Widget Pro is covered by a 24-month warranty from the date of purchase. The
warranty covers manufacturing defects and premature battery degradation, defined
as a cell retaining less than 70 percent of its rated capacity within the warranty
period.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by immersion beyond the IP67 rating,
by opening the housing, or by use of a charger that does not meet the USB-C
power delivery specification. Consumable parts, including the rubber bumper and
the port cover, are excluded.

Service is carried out at regional service centres. Turnaround is normally ten
working days, excluding shipping.

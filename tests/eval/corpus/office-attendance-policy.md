# Office Attendance and Facilities Policy

## Scope

This policy covers the use of Acme Corporation's office facilities by all staff,
including permanent employees, contractors, and visitors. It sits alongside the
remote work policy and does not override it. Where the two appear to conflict,
the remote work policy takes precedence on questions of where an employee works.

## Access

Office access is by staff card. Cards are issued by facilities on the first day
of employment and must be returned on the last. A lost card should be reported
the same day so it can be deactivated; a replacement carries a 25 dollar fee,
deducted from the next pay cycle.

The Wellington office is accessible from six in the morning until ten at night on
weekdays. Weekend access requires a manager's approval, recorded with facilities
in advance. The Auckland and Christchurch offices are accessible during standard
business hours only.

## Desks and meeting rooms

Desks are unassigned. Any desk that has not been claimed by ten in the morning is
available to anyone. Personal belongings must not be left overnight on an
unassigned desk, and facilities will clear anything left after seven in the
evening.

Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar system. A room booked but unused
for ten minutes past the start time is released automatically. The two largest
rooms in the Wellington office may not be booked as recurring meetings without
approval from facilities, because recurring bookings had previously made them
effectively unavailable.

## Visitors

All visitors must be signed in at reception and accompanied by a member of staff
at all times. Visitors are not issued access cards. Staff are responsible for
signing their visitors out on departure.

Contractors working on site for more than four weeks may be issued a temporary
card with the approval of the engaging manager. Temporary cards expire after
ninety days and must be renewed.

## Health and safety

Fire drills are held twice a year at each site. Attendance is mandatory for
anyone in the building. Each floor has two designated wardens, identified by the
notices at each stairwell.

First aid kits are located in the kitchen on each floor. Incidents, including
near misses, must be reported to facilities within twenty-four hours regardless
of whether anyone was injured.

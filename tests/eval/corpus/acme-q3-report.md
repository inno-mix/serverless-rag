# Acme Corporation — Third Quarter Report

## Financial summary

Acme Corporation reported third quarter revenue of 4.2 million dollars, an
increase of 12 percent over the same quarter last year. Gross margin held steady
at 61 percent. Operating expenses rose to 2.6 million dollars, driven mainly by
the expansion of the engineering team and a one-off cost associated with the
Auckland office relocation.

Net profit for the quarter was 380 thousand dollars. The board has elected not to
declare a dividend this quarter, preferring to retain earnings to fund the
product roadmap described in the following section. Cash reserves at the close of
the quarter stood at 5.1 million dollars, giving the company roughly fourteen
months of runway at the current burn rate.

Accounts receivable grew to 1.4 million dollars, which is higher than the board
would like. The finance team has been asked to bring average days sales
outstanding below forty-five days before the end of the financial year.

## Operations

The company operates three regional offices, located in Wellington, Auckland,
and Christchurch. The head office is located in Wellington and houses the
executive team, finance, and the majority of the engineering organisation.

Acme Corporation employs 87 people across all regions. Of these, 34 work in
engineering, 21 in sales and customer success, 18 in operations and support, and
14 in corporate functions including finance, legal, and people operations.
Headcount grew by 9 during the quarter, all of them engineering hires.

Staff turnover for the trailing twelve months was 11 percent, which is below the
industry benchmark of 17 percent for companies of comparable size. Exit
interviews cite compensation as the most common reason for departure, ahead of
career progression and relocation.

## Product roadmap

Three initiatives are funded for the coming two quarters. The first is a rebuild
of the reporting module, which is the most frequent subject of support tickets
and the single largest source of customer dissatisfaction in the most recent
survey. The second is a public API, requested by fourteen enterprise customers
during the last renewal cycle. The third is a mobile client, currently scheduled
to begin after the API work concludes.

The reporting rebuild is expected to take two quarters and is the only initiative
with a dedicated project manager assigned. Engineering leadership has flagged
that the mobile client is unlikely to start before the third quarter of next year
unless additional headcount is approved.

## Risks

The board identified customer concentration as the principal risk to the
business. The three largest customers account for 38 percent of recurring
revenue, and the largest single customer represents 19 percent. The contract with
that customer is due for renewal in February.

A secondary risk is the dependency on a single cloud provider for all production
infrastructure. Management considers the cost of multi-cloud redundancy to
outweigh the benefit at the company's current scale, and has accepted this risk
for the coming financial year.

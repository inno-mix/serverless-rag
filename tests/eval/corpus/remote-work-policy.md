# Remote Work Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Acme Corporation who have
completed their probationary period. Contractors and fixed-term staff are covered
by the terms of their individual engagement and are not subject to this policy.

The policy took effect on the first of March and replaces the interim
arrangements that had been in place since the previous year. It is reviewed
annually by the people operations team.

## Eligibility and arrangement

Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week. The remaining two days
are expected to be worked from the employee's assigned office. Teams may agree on
which days are office days, provided that every team has at least one day per
week when all members are present in person.

Employees who wish to work remotely for more than three days per week must apply
for an exception. Exceptions are approved by the head of people operations, and
require the written support of the employee's direct manager. An approved
exception is valid for six months, after which it must be renewed.

Fully remote arrangements are available only for roles explicitly advertised as
remote. Acme Corporation does not currently employ staff outside New Zealand, and
an approved exception does not extend to working from another country.

## Equipment and expenses

Every remote employee receives a one-off equipment stipend of 1200 dollars, which
may be spent on a desk, a chair, a monitor, or other home office equipment. The
stipend is paid on completion of the probationary period and does not need to be
itemised or receipted.

Laptops and phones are provided by the company and remain company property. They
are replaced on a three-year cycle, or sooner if a device fails. Home internet
costs are not reimbursed, on the basis that the connection is used for personal
purposes as well.

Employees who need specialised equipment for accessibility reasons should contact
people operations directly. Those requests are handled separately from the
stipend and are not capped at 1200 dollars.

## Expectations

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during the core hours of ten in the
morning to three in the afternoon. Outside core hours, staff may arrange their
working day as suits them, provided their commitments are met and their team is
informed.

Meetings that include at least one remote participant should be held entirely
online rather than in a meeting room, so that no participant is disadvantaged by
poor audio. This applies even when most attendees are in the same office.

Managers are asked to hold a one-to-one with each direct report at least
fortnightly. For remote staff this is the primary mechanism for identifying
isolation and workload problems early, and it should not be cancelled without
being rescheduled.

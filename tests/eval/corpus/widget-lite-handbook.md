# Widget Lite — Owner's Handbook

## Overview

The Widget Lite is the entry-level model in the Widget range. It records
temperature and relative humidity, but unlike the Widget Pro it has no barometric
pressure sensor. Readings are stored internally and synchronised to a host
device.

The unit weighs 190 grams and measures 96 by 61 by 24 millimetres. The housing is
moulded polycarbonate. The display is a segment LCD without a backlight, which
keeps power consumption low but limits what can be shown to a single reading at a
time.

## Power

The Widget Lite provides a battery life of 40 hours of continuous logging on a
full charge. Standby time with logging disabled is approximately four months. The
battery is a user-replaceable AA lithium cell, which is the principal practical
difference from the Pro model in field use.

There is no charging circuit; a depleted cell is replaced rather than recharged.
A spare cell is supplied in the box. The low-battery warning appears at 20
percent remaining, and the unit stops logging below 8 percent.

## Environmental limits

The operating temperature range is minus 5 to 45 degrees Celsius, which is
narrower than the Pro model and reflects the polycarbonate housing and the
unheated display. Readings outside this range are not covered by the accuracy
specification.

The unit carries an IP54 rating: protected against dust ingress and splashing
water, but not immersion. Submerging the Widget Lite will damage it and is not
covered by the warranty.

Humidity readings are accurate to plus or minus 4 percent between 20 and 80
percent relative humidity.

## Storage and synchronisation

Internal storage holds 100,000 readings. At the default logging interval of five
minutes, that is approximately 347 days of data. When storage is full the unit
stops logging rather than overwriting, which differs from the Pro model and is
not configurable.

Synchronisation is over the micro-USB connection only. There is no wireless
capability. A full transfer takes about six minutes.

## Warranty and service

The Widget Lite is covered by a 12-month warranty from the date of purchase. The
warranty covers manufacturing defects. Battery cells, being user-replaceable
consumables, are excluded, as are the display window and the battery door seal.

Units are generally replaced rather than repaired, since the cost of service
exceeds the value of the unit in most cases.

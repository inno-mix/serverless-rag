# Acme Corporation — Second Quarter Report

## Financial summary

Acme Corporation reported second quarter revenue of 3.8 million dollars, an
increase of 7 percent over the same quarter last year. Gross margin was 59
percent, slightly below the previous quarter because of a change in the mix of
professional services work.

Operating expenses were 2.4 million dollars. Net profit for the quarter was 290
thousand dollars. The board declared no dividend. Cash reserves at the close of
the quarter stood at 4.7 million dollars.

Accounts receivable stood at 1.1 million dollars at quarter end, with average
days sales outstanding of fifty-two days. The finance team was asked to prepare a
plan to reduce this figure.

## Operations

The company operated two regional offices during the quarter, located in
Wellington and Auckland. The Christchurch office had been approved but had not
yet opened. The head office is located in Wellington.

Acme Corporation employed 78 people at the close of the quarter. Of these, 25
worked in engineering, 20 in sales and customer success, 19 in operations and
support, and 14 in corporate functions. Headcount grew by 4 during the quarter.

Staff turnover for the trailing twelve months was 13 percent. The people
operations team noted that compensation was the most frequently cited reason for
departure in exit interviews.

## Product

Work during the quarter focused on stability rather than new capability. The
support ticket backlog was reduced by 40 percent, and the median time to first
response fell from eleven hours to four hours.

Planning began for the reporting module rebuild, which was subsequently funded in
the following quarter. No new product initiatives were started during the period.

## Risks

The board noted customer concentration as a growing concern, with the three
largest customers accounting for 35 percent of recurring revenue at the time of
review. The board asked management to report on diversification options at the
next meeting.
